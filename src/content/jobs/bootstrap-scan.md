## The 24-Hour Security Wake-Up Call
Not ready for a full audit? The "Bootstrap" Scan identifies the "low-hanging fruit" that attackers use to target small businesses. Fast, affordable, and actionable.
### What You Receive:
- **External Asset Scan:** Coverage of up to 5 external IP addresses or domains.
- **Critical Risk Summary:** A 1-page executive briefing of the top 3 vulnerabilities you need to fix right now.
- **Raw Technical Logs:** Detailed output for your developers to use in remediation.
- **Rapid Delivery:** Results delivered within 24 hours of kickoff.
### The Value:
The most affordable way to see what an attacker sees. Perfect for founders preparing for their first enterprise security questionnaire.
### Onboarding & Verification
To ensure all security work is legally authorized, this 24-hour service requires:
- **Technical Proof of Ownership:** You must add a unique verification token to your domain's DNS (TXT record) or host a small file on the target server.
- **Authorized Requestor:** Requests must originate from an official company email address.
- **Right to Audit:** Kickoff requires a signed Task Order containing a formal Right to Audit attestation.
- **Payment Verification:** Initial payment must be processed through a verified business account or corporate credit card to finalize the kickoff.
