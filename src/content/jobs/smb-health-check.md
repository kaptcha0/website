## Find the Gaps Before They Are Exploited
A "Health Check" is a fixed-scope engagement for businesses that need a point-in-time snapshot of their risks. I look at your systems the way an attacker would—from the outside in.
### What is Included:
- **External Reconnaissance:** Scanning for open ports and exposed services.
- **Internal Discovery:** Identifying unpatched workstations and misconfigured hardware.
- **Credential Audit:** Checking for default passwords and orphaned accounts.
- **Remediation Roadmap:** A prioritized report ranking risks by impact and providing actionable fix steps.
### The Value:
I provide the "discovery" phase of a penetration test without the enterprise price tag. You receive a clear list of what to fix first to maximize your security ROI.
### Onboarding & Verification
This engagement involves diagnostic discovery of your network and requires formal business verification:
- **Business Identity Check:** I verify the requesting entity against state public records (e.g., Virginia SCC) to ensure it is in good standing.
- **Asset Authorization:** Clients must provide a signed list of authorized IP ranges and domains, certifying legal control over all targets.
- **Payment Verification:** Initial deposits must be processed through a verified business account or corporate credit card.
