## Secure Your Ecosystem from Day One
Most businesses buy software but don't know how to secure it. This engagement ensures your platform is configured as a "fortress" before your first real user ever logs in.
### What is Included:
- **Identity Hardening:** Enforcement of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and Single Sign-On (SSO) integration (SAML/OIDC).
- **Least-Privilege RBAC:** Designing custom Role-Based Access Control so users only see what they need.
- **Data Exposure Audit:** Configuring sharing rules to prevent accidental data leaks.
- **Custom Setup Guides:** Professional documentation tailored specifically to your team’s workflow.
### The Value:
This is a **White Box** engagement where I act as your technical architect. We build the "paved path" for your employees, ensuring that security doesn't slow them down—it protects them.
### Onboarding & Verification
Because this engagement involves administrative access to your core business platforms, the following safeguards are in place:
- **Access Scoping:** We use the "Least Privilege" model; I only request the specific administrative permissions needed for the hardening phase.
- **Verification of Authority:** The requestor must demonstrate administrative control over the SaaS instance (e.g., Google Workspace or Okta) before configuration begins.
- **Safe Harbor:** All implementation work is performed under a signed agreement that defines the boundaries of the "Blue Team" technical audit.
- **Payment Verification:** Service retainers or deposits must originate from a verified corporate financial institution to ensure business alignment.
